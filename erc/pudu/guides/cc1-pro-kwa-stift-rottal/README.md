# KWA Stift Rottal CC1 Pro

Customer-specific German operating and care guide. Keep this page separate from `cc1-pro-kwa-konstanz` and the generic quick guide.

Published content covers wet cleaning of wood and resilient floors, task power settings, system cleaning-speed inheritance, the AI adaptive-cleaning switch, water-distributor and filter care, and workstation wastewater-inlet flushing.

## Sources and editorial scope

- PUDU Academy CC1 Pro Operation Guide V2.0: `https://academy.pudutech.com/en/docs/PUDU-CC1-Pro-Operation-Guide-V2`
- Workstation manual V4.0: `https://academy.pudutech.com/zh/docs/gong-zuo-zhan-yong-hu-shou-ce-User-Manual-V4`
- Workstation maintenance: `https://academy.pudutech.com/en/docs/CC1-gong-zuo-zhan-xuan-pei-bao-yang-zhi-nan`
- Operator-specific instructions provided by Xi Wang on 2026-10-19: three task parameter levels; task opt-in to system cleaning speed; approximately 60 C water only for the taught wastewater-inlet flush, weekly; tank-wall cleaning every 3-5 days if automatic water exchange works.
- The V4.0 manual confirms steady blue standby, flashing green connecting, steady green connected, flashing blue forced charging without a connection, flashing red fault. The older English introduction differs and must not override V4.0.

The 60 C procedure is a site instruction, not a manufacturer temperature specification. Do not transfer it to the robot's fresh-water tank. Actual customer incident reports, personal contact details and robot serial numbers are deliberately outside this public operating guide.

## Screenshots still needed

Capture these on the actual CC1 Pro with German selected. Existing portal images do not show these screens. Menu paths below describe the intended destination; exact German submenu text still needs to be confirmed from the device.

1. Startseite > Automatisch reinigen > select an existing wet-cleaning task: show the task name and the Bearbeiten control. This provides the entry point into the parameter editor.
2. That task > Bearbeiten > Reinigungsparameter > Nassreinigung (Scrubbing): show all three levels for roller speed, water amount and suction, plus the save/confirm control. If these cannot fit on one screen, capture the upper and lower parts separately.
3. Startseite > Einstellungen > Geschwindigkeit (Speed) > Nassreinigung: show the actual cleaning-speed control, its unit and save/confirm control; distinguish it from transit speed.
4. Back in the task's cleaning parameters: show the checkbox or switch that adopts the system cleaning speed, its full label and surrounding task-speed controls. This may be combined with item 2 if both fit legibly.
5. Startseite > Einstellungen > Reinigungseinstellungen (Cleaning Settings): show the adaptive AI-cleaning switch (AI adaptive cleaning) and the separate obstacle-recognition switch. Capture before changing the setting; no adjustment is required just for a screenshot.

Optional: a front-on photo of the installed workstation showing the taught central wastewater receiving opening, taken with the robot moved away. The existing `arbeitsstation.png` shows a menu, not this physical flushing point.
