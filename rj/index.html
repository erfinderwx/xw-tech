Scope

Mechanical conversion, battery installation, control units and complete vehicle harness routing.Confirm that the vehicle hardware and connector layout match the configuration shown before beginning.

Read before work begins

Safety is a required first step

Qualified technicians only. This procedure includes battery, controller and power-cable work.

Switch off the vehicle, disconnect external charging and follow the approved high-voltage isolation procedure.

Prevent unexpected vehicle movement and protect cables, painted surfaces and nearby components during cutting or grinding.

Verify connector labels, keying and terminal markings. If the vehicle differs from the videos, stop and obtain the approved service information.

01

Procedure · 02:54

Replace the underbody protection plates

Replace the front and rear protection plates, add the supplied spacers and complete the final torque check.

Important

The source narration specifies a final torque of 12 N·m. Confirm that this value matches the approved hardware supplied for the vehicle before tightening.

  1. 01

    Remove the original plates

    Remove the existing fasteners and lower the protection plates. Keep the front and rear plate positions clearly identified.

  2. 02

    Prepare the replacement hardware

    Use the M6 × 30 T30 Torx fasteners shown in the video. Position a spacer between the plate and frame at each specified center mounting point.

  3. 03

    Fit and align

    Apply thread-locking compound to each required fastener. Support the plate, start all fasteners and check that the edges sit clear of the frame and nearby components.

  4. 04

    Tighten and torque

    Snug the fasteners with the powered tool, then use a torque wrench for the final 12 N·m tightening pass. Do not use the powered tool for the final torque check.

Procedure · 00:55

Replace the battery tray

Transfer the support pads and washers, prepare the specified fixing point and install the replacement tray.

Important

This stage includes cutting and grinding one specific rear-center fixing point. Confirm the exact target shown in the video before making any irreversible modification. Do not alter the other mounting studs.

  1. 01

    Remove the old tray

    Lift the original battery tray from the vehicle and place it where its support parts can be transferred without being lost.

  2. 02

    Transfer the supports

    Remove the rubber pads and flat washers shown in the video. Inspect them before reuse and keep their installed order.

  3. 03

    Prepare the center fixing point

    Remove only the rear-center protruding fixing point identified in the video, then finish the remaining surface smooth. Protect nearby wiring and painted surfaces while cutting or grinding.

  4. 04

    Verify and install

    Confirm that the five required mounting studs, rubber pads and flat washers are present, then lower the replacement tray into position without trapping a cable or pad.

03

Procedure · 00:35

Install the new battery

Lower the new battery assembly into the tray, align all five points and secure it evenly.

Important

The source video does not specify a tightening torque for this stage. Use the approved service value for the supplied battery hardware.

  1. 01

    Prepare the tray

    Check that the five mounting locations are clear and that no harness, washer or support pad can be trapped beneath the battery.

  2. 02

    Lower the battery

    Use an appropriate handling method to lower the new battery assembly squarely into the tray. Keep hands and cables clear of pinch points.

  3. 03

    Align five points

    Move the assembly only as required until all five mounting points line up without forcing the battery against the tray.

  4. 04

    Secure evenly

    Start each fixing before tightening. Tighten progressively around the assembly so the battery remains flat and centered.

Procedure · 02:34

Install the VCU, DC assembly and harness

Mount the new harness and control assemblies, connect the matching plugs and secure the complete routing path.

Important

Make every connection with the electrical system isolated. Match connector labels, keying and latch position; never force a connector that does not seat freely.

  1. 01

    Position the harness assembly

    Place the new harness assembly in the battery compartment and align it with the five mounting points shown.

  2. 02

    Secure the five points

    Install the M6 nuts and tighten the assembly evenly. Keep the harness clear of the nut faces and tool path.

  3. 03

    Install the control bracket

    Position the combined VCU and DC assembly bracket in the orientation shown in the video, then engage its mounting locations.

  4. 04

    Connect the control units

    Connect the labeled harness plugs to their matching VCU and DC assembly interfaces. Confirm each latch is fully engaged.

  5. 05

    Mount and route

    Secure the assembly at its two fixing points. Route the harness along the demonstrated path and lock each clip into its assigned hole.

05

Procedure · 03:19

Install the rear-drive harness

Route the rear power leads, connect both drive sides and complete the motor and vehicle-harness interfaces.

Important

Verify terminal markings and left/right routing on the components before tightening. Do not determine polarity from cable colour alone.

  1. 01

    Position the main harness

    Lay the initial power-harness section in the position shown and secure it with the supplied clamp and M6 fixing.

  2. 02

    Route to both rear drives

    Lead the rear-drive power cables to the left and right drive areas. Maintain the demonstrated bend radius and keep the cables clear of suspension and steering movement.

  3. 03

    Orient the cable lugs

    Arrange the paired cable lugs in the orientation shown, with the adjacent lugs turned approximately 90 degrees to avoid overlap and stress.

  4. 04

    Connect left and right drive wiring

    Open the protective covers and attach the corresponding leads to the marked terminals on each side. Refit the terminal protection after tightening.

  5. 05

    Complete the signal connections

    Connect the left and right motor harnesses and mate the three-pin connector to the vehicle harness. Secure the finished routing with the demonstrated ties.

Procedure · 06:12

Install the charging and powered-trailer harnesses

Install both rear interfaces, route the harnesses along the frame and complete the battery-side connections.

Important

The charging and trailer-power interfaces are not interchangeable. Confirm the connector label, keying and battery-port designation before insertion.

  1. 01

    Route the trailer harness

    Feed the powered-trailer harness through the indicated frame opening toward the rear of the vehicle.

  2. 02

    Mount the trailer connector

    Install the connector in the rear metal bracket with the orientation shown. Route the harness along the frame and secure every supplied clamp.

  3. 03

    Install the charging harness

    Route the charging harness from the front section to the rear and mount the charging inlet in the demonstrated position.

  4. 04

    Secure the signal branch

    Install the DR-harness signal connector at its mounting position and secure the charging and DR harnesses together along the shared route.

  5. 05

    Make the battery-side connections

    Connect the charging harness to the matching battery charge interface and the trailer-power leads to their matching output interface. Push each connector until its lock is positively engaged.

  6. 06

    Close the compartment

    Arrange the internal harnesses without tight bends or trapped sections. Refit the compartment covers and tighten all four cover fixing points.

Procedure · 11:04

Install the steering-column and front-drive harnesses

Pull the four-branch harness through the column, connect the handlebar equipment and reinstall both front-drive controllers.

Important

Before tightening a controller terminal, read the B+ and B− marks on that controller. Stop if the component labels or cable colours differ from the configuration shown in the source video.

  1. 01

    Install the guide line

    Feed a guide line from the upper steering-column opening to the lower opening. Join it securely to the four-branch DR harness with PVC electrical tape.

  2. 02

    Pull the harness through

    Pull slowly while gently rotating the guide line. Feed the branches into the column without scraping, kinking or pulling directly on a connector.

  3. 03

    Route the lower section

    Remove the tape and guide line, organize the lower branches and pass them into the electrical compartment outlet.

  4. 04

    Ground, clamp and seal

    Secure the ground lug, install the supplied column clamp and fit the lower grommet fully into the opening.

  5. 05

    Connect the upper branches

    Using the harness labels, connect the left brake, right brake, instrument cluster, left switch, ignition switch and throttle branches to their matching plugs.

  6. 06

    Complete the column

    Route and connect the key switch, then return the connectors into the column one at a time. Fit the upper grommet without pinching the harness.

  7. 07

    Connect the front-drive power leads

    On the high-voltage pair shown, connect red to B+ and blue to B−. On the harness power branch shown, connect red to B+ and black to B−. Verify the controller markings before every connection.

  8. 08

    Reconnect the controllers

    Restore the terminal protection, connect the DR plug until it locks, and mate the controller motor interfaces to the corresponding motor harness.

  9. 09

    Reinstall and inspect

    Install the right and left controllers in their respective housings, secure their mounting points and verify full steering movement without harness tension or rubbing.

Before continuing

  • Both column grommets are fully seated
  • All upper connectors match their harness labels
  • Controller polarity was verified from B+/B− markings
  • Steering moves lock-to-lock without pulling or rubbing a harness

Final inspection

Verify before power-up

  1. 01

    Confirm that the battery, tray, control units, covers and protection plates are mechanically secure.

  2. 02

    Check every connector latch, grommet, terminal cover, routing clip and cable tie.

  3. 03

    Recheck B+ and B− connections against the component markings and approved wiring information.

  4. 04

    Move the steering through its full range and inspect all four wheel areas for cable clearance.

  5. 05

    Complete electrical checks and commissioning only in accordance with the approved RJ Tech service procedure.
