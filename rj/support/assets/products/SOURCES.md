# Product card image sources

These images illustrate the existing product entrances. Images do not change the availability or scope of support resources.

- `steinadler-pro.webp`: official Steinadler Pro L7e product image, downloaded on 2026-10-10 from https://www.rjtech-offroad.com/admin/theme/RJTECH/images/l7ea1/appearce-1-1.png (product page: https://www.rjtech-offroad.com/en/L7e_A1).
- `luchs-a.webp`: Luchs A-4WS IP54 render supplied by the site owner in `Luchs_Family_External_20261009.zip`, at `Luchs_A/images/Luchs_A-4WS_IP54_render_iso_fl.png`. It depicts the A-4WS configuration.
- `luchs-b.webp`: official Luchs B product image, downloaded on 2026-10-10 from https://www.rjtech-offroad.com/Upfile/EditPage/20260323/6ffffe7f-89eb-4109-a4e2-426760d43739.webp (product page: https://www.rjtech-offroad.com/en/4*4?v=1).

Images were resized proportionally to 900 pixels wide and encoded as WebP at quality 85, with transparency preserved. Vehicles are not cropped or altered. The original external ZIP remains archived outside the repository. Public document and integration-file provenance is recorded in `files/luchs/SOURCES.json`.
