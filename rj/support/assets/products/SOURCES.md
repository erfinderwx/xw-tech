# Product card image sources

These images illustrate the existing product entrances. They do not change the availability or scope of support resources. All three cards use rendered views facing right, transparent 1200 × 800 WebP frames and proportional resizing. Luchs B has a larger image footprint than Luchs A; the cards are not dimensioned drawings to a common scale.

## V2.2 images

- `steinadler-pro.webp`: official Steinadler Pro L7e three-quarter render, frame 60 of the public product spin. [Original image](https://www.rjtech-offroad.com/admin/theme/RJTECH/images/l7ea1/animation/60.png), [product page](https://www.rjtech-offroad.com/en/L7e_A1). This replaces the earlier left-facing side view without mirroring the vehicle.
- `luchs-a.webp`: the existing Luchs A-4WS IP54 render supplied by the site owner in `Luchs_Family_External_20261009.zip`, at `Luchs_A/images/Luchs_A-4WS_IP54_render_iso_fl.png`. Its geometry, materials and colours are retained. Only the image frame and proportional presentation size change.
- `luchs-b.webp`: a new exterior raster render of the owner-confirmed Luchs B chassis model. Retain the native body dimensions, deck boundary, seams and fittings. Use black coated bodywork, rubber tyres and metallic wheel/mechanical finishes. Tread is a surface-shading detail, without changing the native tyre outline. Sensors, antennas and the decorative product light strip are omitted at the owner's request.

B's physical paint and material references are the [public black-product photograph](https://www.rjtech-offroad.com/Upfile/EditPage/20260703/6f8f811b-1b52-4e5a-9de6-a31f781c8dc6.jpg) and [side photograph](https://www.rjtech-offroad.com/Upfile/EditPage/20260703/f1ef35e2-936a-4dd2-bfd1-bcc4bd56cc49.jpg). The [earlier public product image](https://www.rjtech-offroad.com/Upfile/EditPage/20260323/6ffffe7f-89eb-4109-a4e2-426760d43739.webp) informs tyre, metal and red mechanical finishes only; it does not override the native chassis shape or supply product lighting.

Vehicles are not stretched or mirrored. Transparent padding and a shared baseline align the images within the existing responsive cards. Assets use WebP quality 92. Earlier website assets remain available in the immutable release snapshots linked from `CHANGELOG.md`.

The external ZIP, B model, render scenes and review records remain outside the public repository. Only the final raster image is published for B. This image does not make a B engineering model available in Developer downloads. Public document and integration-file provenance remains in `files/luchs/SOURCES.json`.
